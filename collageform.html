DYPCMR

Dr. D. Y. Patil Centre for Management and Research (DYPCMR), established in 2008 in Chikhali, Pune, is a premier management institution dedicated to shaping future industry leaders. Affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), approved by AICTE, and accredited with a NAAC B++ grade, the college offers high-quality postgraduate programs, primarily focusing on its master's tracks in business and technology. Strategically located near major industrial and IT hubs, the campus features state-of-the-art infrastructure, including technologically advanced classrooms, extensive computer labs, a well-stocked library, and full Wi-Fi connectivity. By emphasizing a student-centric teaching pedagogy, regular corporate interactions, and practical placement drives, DYPCMR successfully bridges the gap between academic education and modern global business requirements.
